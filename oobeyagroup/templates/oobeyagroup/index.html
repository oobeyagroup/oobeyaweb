Oobeya Group founder, Lowell Lindstrom, brings 40 years of professional experience in software development to tailor services to each customer. For nearly 20 years, Oobeya Group has specialized in helping technology leaders enhance their teams' skills and organizational capabilities. Our expertise improves technology leadership, product management, and software development competencies.

Agile & Scrum

Lowell was part of the team that incubated the Agile movement. He is responsible for many Agile firsts, including: launching the first agile developer course in 1999, authoring the first product owner course in 2000, and co-chairing first the North American Agile conference in 2001 (now Agile 20xx). This experience is reflected in our services. Unlike the mass market dogma that has failed many companies, we help you succeed with agile practices grounded in early pragmatic principles.
